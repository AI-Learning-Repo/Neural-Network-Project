# Vellamo & Pine Atelier: The Solvik Archipelago 7-Day Trip Planner

## 1. Overview & Seasonal Travel Windows
The Solvik Archipelago is a remote sub-arctic volcanic island chain consisting of three main inhabited islands: Vespera, Kaldor, and Tarnhollow. All itineraries planned by Vellamo & Pine Atelier follow strict maritime and ecological windows. Standard commercial GPS maps do not display seasonal trail closures or private shuttle routes across the archipelago, so travelers must follow this official route guide.

The travel year is divided into three operational seasons:
- **Frostmelt Season (April 10 – June 15):** Lower valleys are accessible, though high ridges on Tarnhollow remain snowbound. Expect daytime temperatures between 4°C and 11°C.
- **Amberleaf Window (June 16 – September 20):** The only window when all three islands, the Mosslight Caves, and the outer sea stacks are fully open.
- **Twilight Freeze (September 21 – November 10):** Limited ferry schedules apply, and solo hiking above 300 meters is prohibited without a certified beacon.

## 2. Days 1–2: Arrival in Port Brine & Vespera Island
Every itinerary begins at **Port Brine** on the southern tip of Vespera Island. Travelers arriving by seaplane must check in at **Harbor Kiosk B** within 45 minutes of landing to validate their physical entry badge. From Port Brine, private cars are banned on Vespera's coastal road. Instead, guests board the **Blue Heron Electric Tram**, which departs strictly at **06:45, 11:10, and 14:20**. 

On Day 1 afternoon, walk the Whispering Cliffs boardwalk (3.4 km, easy grade) ending at Saltmill Square. Stop at the **Copper Kettle Bakery**, which opens exclusively from 15:00 to 17:30 and serves spiced sea-buckthorn tarts reserved for Vellamo & Pine guests. 

On Day 2, explore the Vespera Botanical Basalt Gardens. Entrance is included in your itinerary, provided you present your paper voucher at the North Turnstile. Note that drones are strictly banned across Vespera due to nesting puffin colonies; unauthorized flights incur an immediate 350 Solvik Credit fine.

## 3. Days 3–4: Crossing to Kaldor & The Obsidian Baths
On the morning of Day 3, travelers cross the Frostwind Strait from Vespera to Kaldor Island aboard the **Silver Birch Ferry**. Under standard operating schedules, the Silver Birch Ferry only runs on Tuesdays and Fridays, departing Vespera East Pier at 08:15 sharp and arriving at Kaldor Cove at 09:40. 

However, during the annual Crimson Tide Festival held during the second week of October, that same ferry switches to a daily midnight schedule and requires travelers to enter Pass Code CTF-90 at the boarding gate. Travelers who miss the ferry cannot hire private fishing boats to cross the strait, as maritime law restricts passenger crossings to licensed hull operators.

Once on Kaldor, Day 3 afternoon is dedicated to the **Kaldor Obsidian Baths**, a natural geothermal pool complex carved into black volcanic rock. Guests must adhere to three mandatory bath rules:
- Maximum soaking time is strictly 45 minutes per session to prevent thermal fatigue.
- Synthetic swimwear and jewelry are prohibited due to high sulfur mineralization; complimentary organic linen wraps are issued at **Gate C**.
- Footpaths between the changing pavilions and Pool 2 are unlit after 19:00, so guests staying for the twilight slot must carry a red-filter headlamp.

Day 4 features the **Black-Sand Ridge Loop** (11.2 km, moderate). Lunch is packed by your lodge and includes smoked char flatbread. Water refill stations are located at Kilometer 4 and Kilometer 8, though the Kilometer 8 pump is shut off after October 1 to prevent pipe freezing.

## 4. Days 5–6: Tarnhollow Highlands & Mosslight Caves
Day 5 begins with a 35-minute hovercraft transfer to Tarnhollow, the most rugged island in the Solvik chain. The highlight of Day 5 is the guided descent into the **Mosslight Caves**, famous for bioluminescent lichen along the cavern ceiling. Independent entry is forbidden. Travelers must be accompanied by Chief Navigator **Henrik Lindqvist** or one of his three licensed deputies.

To enter the cavern system, each traveler must collect a physical brass **Glow-Lamp Token** from the Tarnhollow Rangers' Outpost before **11:00 AM** on the day of the tour. Tokens are non-transferable and must be returned to the Rangers' Outpost drop-box by 17:30. Camera flash photography is banned inside the caves because bright UV bursts bleach the bioluminescent lichen for up to six months.

Day 6 is reserved for the **Sunken Caldera Summit Trail** (14.8 km, strenuous, 720 meters elevation gain). Hikers must sign the logbook at Trailhead Marker 1 before 08:30. Any hiker who has not signed back in by 18:00 triggers an automatic search dispatch billed to the traveler's contingency deposit.

## 5. Day 7: Return to Port Brine & Customs Rules
On the final morning, the express catamaran departs Tarnhollow North Dock at 07:50, reaching Port Brine at 09:30 in time for midday seaplane departures. Before passing through Port Brine Departure Control, travelers must declare restricted local goods:
- **Volcanic Obsidian & Basalt Samples:** Strictly limited to 400 grams per traveler, and only stones collected from designated quarry bins at Kaldor Cove are permitted.
- **Artisanal Amber Honey:** Jars over 150 ml must be declared using **Customs Form SH-12**, available at Harbor Kiosk B.
- **Hand-knitted Driftwool:** Duty-free up to 3 items per adult passport.
