# Vellamo & Pine Atelier: Partner Lodges, Dining & Emergency Protocols

## 1. Partner Accommodations Directory
Vellamo & Pine Atelier partners exclusively with three locally owned eco-properties across the archipelago. Travelers are assigned rooms according to their island rotation, and private check-in instructions are sent 48 hours before arrival.

### The Basalt Lantern Inn (Vespera Island)
Located 600 meters north of Saltmill Square, The Basalt Lantern Inn features 18 rooms heated by closed-loop geothermal floor coils. Quiet hours are strictly enforced from **21:30 to 06:30**. The inn operates without a 24-hour front desk; instead, the main cedar door uses a digital keypad whose 6-digit access code resets every Monday at noon and is delivered via encrypted SMS to the lead traveler's phone. 

Breakfast is served in the Glass Atrium from 06:30 to 09:30, though the kitchen's signature smoked cloudberry porridge is only served until **08:45** sharp. Boot-drying lockers are located in the basement mudroom and use room-number brass keys.

### Pinecrest Timber Cabins (Kaldor Island)
Situated beside a sheltered birch cove, Pinecrest consists of 10 off-grid cabins operating under a mandatory **Digital Silence Policy**. There is **no public Wi-Fi** and cellular repeaters are disabled within the property perimeter to preserve the dark-sky sanctuary. 

In case guests need to send urgent messages to family or trip planners, a single satellite text terminal is housed inside **Cabin 0 (the Warden's Office)** and is available between 17:00 and 19:00 daily. Guests may reserve the wood-fired shoreline sauna in 50-minute private slots by writing their cabin number on the slate chalkboard hanging at the pier.

### Tarnhollow Cliffside Refuge (Tarnhollow Island)
Built directly into a basalt ledge overlooking the northern sea stacks, Tarnhollow Cliffside Refuge is powered 100% by rooftop solar arrays and micro-wind turbines. Because the refuge runs on a delicate **1,200W micro-inverter**, high-draw appliances such as hairdryers, curling irons, and personal electric kettles are strictly prohibited; plugging them in trips the main breaker for the entire west wing.

Potable drinking water is drawn from **Glacial Cistern B**. While filtered year-round, a mandatory 3-minute boil advisory applies to Cistern B water during the month of August due to seasonal glacial silt runoff.

## 2. Culinary Reservations & Dietary Protocols
Dining options across the Solvik Archipelago are limited, and walk-in seating is rarely available during Amberleaf Window.

- **Restaurant Salt & Ember (Port Brine):** The archipelago's premier wood-hearth dining room only accepts reservations submitted through an official Vellamo & Pine planner code (**NA-DINE-77**). Notably, Salt & Ember is closed every **Wednesday** and on **nights of the full moon**, when the kitchen staff closes early for coastal seaweed and herb foraging.
- **Dietary & Allergy Logging:** All severe allergies and dietary restrictions must be logged inside the Trip Planner Portal at least **10 days prior** to Day 1 arrival. Because supply barges arrive only twice per month, last-minute dietary accommodations cannot be guaranteed on Kaldor or Tarnhollow.
- **The Green-Leaf Guarantee:** All three partner lodges maintain a dedicated shellfish-free and pine-nut-free prep station and can serve the standardized **Green-Leaf Menu** for vegan and celiac travelers if pre-registered on time.

## 3. Storm Contingency & Emergency Protocols
Weather in the sub-arctic Atlantic changes rapidly. Travelers and trip planners must memorize the following contingency rules:

### Storm Contingency Route: Protocol Gull
When maritime winds exceed **55 knots**, all surface vessels—including the Silver Birch Ferry and the Tarnhollow hovercraft—are immediately grounded. If guests are stranded on Kaldor Island when **Protocol Gull** is activated, local rangers evacuate travelers back to Vespera via the **Underground Maintenance Rail (Tunnel 4)**, a single-track geothermal service tunnel linking Kaldor Cove to Port Brine. Evacuation departures run every 2 hours during daylight, and passengers must wear the reflective vests stored under every railcar bench.

### Emergency Communications & SOS Contacts
Because standard cellular networks drop out in deep fjords and on Tarnhollow trails, every Tier 2 and Tier 3 group is issued a waterproof VHF handset tuned to:
- **Primary Emergency Channel:** Maritime Channel **16-Alt**
- **Backup Regional Channel:** Channel **72**
- **24/7 Satellite SOS Desk (Solvik Watchtower):** **+990-44-819-200**

### Lost Permit Replacement Procedure
If a traveler loses their physical paper copy of Permit SA-2026-ALPHA or SA-2026-OMEGA while on the islands, they must visit the **Brineholm Harbor Kiosk** (or ask the Cabin 0 Warden on Kaldor to transmit a request). A temporary 48-hour digital pass can be reissued upon presenting an original passport alongside the **last 4 digits of the Vellamo & Pine booking reference**.
