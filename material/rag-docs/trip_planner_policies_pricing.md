# Vellamo & Pine Atelier: Internal Booking, Permits & Pricing Handbook (2026)

## 1. Trip Tier Packages & Base Pricing
All bookings for the Solvik Archipelago are quoted in both **Solvik Credits (SC)** and Euros. Prices below reflect per-person rates based on double occupancy for the 7-day standard circuit:

- **Tier 1 — Driftwood Explorer (1,450 SC / €1,890):** Includes standard ferry crossings, 3-star guesthouse lodging, daily breakfast, and Vespera/Kaldor entry permits. Does not include the Tarnhollow hovercraft or guided Mosslight Caves descent.
- **Tier 2 — Basalt Ridge (2,300 SC / €2,950):** Includes all Tier 1 benefits plus Tarnhollow hovercraft transfers, Mosslight Caves guide fees, packed trail lunches, and Priority Gate C access at the Kaldor Obsidian Baths.
- **Tier 3 — Aurora Crown Private (4,100 SC / €5,200):** Includes private seaplane charter scheduling, dedicated personal navigator, single-occupancy luxury cliff cabins, and reserved table access at Restaurant Salt & Ember.

Solo travelers booking Tier 1 or Tier 2 incur a mandatory 35% single-supplement surcharge unless they opt into the Shared Cabin Matching Program at least 30 days prior to departure.

## 2. Mandatory Archipelago Entry Permits
Because 78% of the Solvik Archipelago is a protected biosphere reserve, visitors cannot purchase tickets on arrival. Travel planners must register two distinct permits in the Archipelago Registry Portal:

1. **Permit SA-2026-ALPHA:** Covers Vespera and Kaldor Island access, Blue Heron Electric Tram passes, and coastal trail usage. Required for all tiers.
2. **Permit SA-2026-OMEGA:** Required additionally for travelers entering Tarnhollow Island and the Mosslight Caves. Only 48 Omega permits are issued per day across all tour operators.

Standard permit processing requires **19 business days** prior to the arrival date. If a client requests a late booking between 7 and 18 business days before departure, planners may apply for Expedited Processing for a non-refundable rush fee of **€145 per person**. Applications submitted fewer than 7 business days before arrival are automatically rejected by the Solvik Registry.

## 3. Seaplane & Luggage Regulations
Inbound and outbound transfers between the mainland and Port Brine operate exclusively on **Skylark-4 twin-propeller seaplanes**. Weight and balance limits are strictly enforced on the dock prior to boarding:

- **Maximum Luggage Weight:** Each passenger is restricted to **14 kg** of checked baggage plus **4 kg** in a soft personal daypack.
- **Prohibited Bag Types:** Hard-shell roller suitcases are strictly banned because they cannot fit into the curved tail cargo bay of the Skylark-4. Travelers arriving with hard-shell bags must transfer their belongings into complimentary canvas duffels at the mainland terminal.
- **Battery & Electronics Rule:** Spare lithium batteries exceeding **100Wh** are prohibited on board. Drone batteries must be stored inside fire-retardant LiPo bags in the cabin.
- **Excess Storage:** Any luggage exceeding the 14 kg limit is held at **Port Brine Locker Vault #7** for a flat storage fee of **€18 per day**, payable by card only.

## 4. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Weather Refunds
Vellamo & Pine Atelier applies a tiered cancellation schedule calculated from the scheduled arrival date at Port Brine:

- **45+ Days Prior:** 90% cash refund minus a €65 administrative processing fee, or 100% conversion to a transferable travel credit valid for 24 months.
- **15 to 44 Days Prior:** 50% cash refund, or 100% conversion to a non-transferable travel voucher valid for 18 months.
- **0 to 14 Days Prior:** Strictly non-refundable. No cash or voucher compensation is issued for personal illness, missed mainland flights, or change of plans.

**The Level-3 Gale Protocol Exception:** If the Solvik Maritime Authority declares a **Level-3 Gale Protocol** (sustained winds above 55 knots for more than 12 hours), standard cancellation penalties are waived. Affected guests automatically receive free rebooking onto the next available departure window plus a **€120 per-person meal and lodging stipend** for mainland delays.

## 5. Loyalty & Early-Bird Discounts
Returning travelers who hold an active **Compass Key ID** (issued after completing at least one previous Solvik itinerary) receive a **12% loyalty discount** on Tier 2 and Tier 3 packages. First-time travelers booking before January 31 for summer departures may apply promotional code **SOLVIK-EARLY15** for a 15% reduction on Tier 1 or Tier 2. 

Note that the Compass Key loyalty discount and the SOLVIK-EARLY15 code cannot be stacked or combined on the same invoice. Corporate or academic research groups of 6 or more travelers qualify for a flat 10% group waiver on Permit SA-2026-ALPHA fees when invoiced under a single institutional purchase order.
