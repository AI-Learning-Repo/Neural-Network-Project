# Sprint 1: Jigsaw Voting Guide

Read this guide immediately before answering the Zoom poll.

Voting takes place after all mixed-group Jigsaw breakout rooms close and participants return to the **main Zoom room**.

## 1. Check the room list before voting

Record the project group numbers represented in the assigned mixed-group room:

- **Participant's project group:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 1:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 2:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 3:** `[Group #]`

If the room had fewer than four participants, list only the groups actually represented.

The poll lists **Group 1 through Group 7**. Only groups represented by **other students in the participant's assigned Jigsaw room** are eligible. The participant's own project group is ineligible.

## 2. Zoom poll question 1: Best Presentation

**Question type:** Single choice: select **exactly one** group.

> **Which project group delivered the best Sprint 1 presentation in the assigned Jigsaw breakout room?**
>
> Consider clarity, a working assistant or agent, the demonstration, tool/database/API integration, evaluation, Scrum explanation, and answers to technical questions.

Choose one eligible group whose presentation was observed in the assigned room.

## 3. Zoom poll question 2: Concept Connectors

**Question type:** Multiple choice: select **exactly two distinct** groups.

> **Which TWO project groups were represented by the strongest Concept Connectors in the assigned Jigsaw breakout room?**
>
> Consider representatives who asked useful technical questions, connected ideas across projects, highlighted limitations, or clarified how AI assistants and agents work.

Choose two eligible groups represented in that room. A useful question can contribute more to understanding than a complex question that does not clarify the project.

## 4. Final check before submitting

- [ ] Exactly **one group** is selected for Best Presentation.
- [ ] Exactly **two distinct groups** are selected for Concept Connectors.
- [ ] Every selected group had a representative in the assigned Jigsaw room.
- [ ] The participant's own project group is excluded from both questions.
- [ ] Each selected group number matches the room record.

If fewer than two other groups are eligible, inform the instructor rather than selecting an absent group. Report a mistake promptly if it is discovered after submission.

## 5. How votes are used

The instructor checks eligibility before calculating scores. Only valid votes contribute to the competition.

Each group's vote counts are divided by its **eligible voting opportunities**, so groups with more representatives do not gain an automatic advantage. With four participants in every room, the denominator is **three other potential voters per representative**. The scoring formula is:

$$
\boxed{\text{Jigsaw Score} = 5\left(\frac{\text{Valid Presentation Votes}}{\text{Eligible Voting Opportunities}}\right) + 3\left(\frac{\text{Valid Connector Votes}}{\text{Eligible Voting Opportunities}}\right)}
$$

The maximum score is **8**. The coefficients **5** and **3** are competition weights, **not course grade points**. Recognition of the winning group uses validated normalized scores. Any course bonus connected to a badge is determined separately.

## 6. Voting privacy

Individual choices are confidential among classmates. Other students are not shown individual answers. The instructor can inspect identifiable records to check self-votes, selections of groups absent from a room, and other voting errors.

The poll is therefore not anonymous to the instructor. Only aggregate group results are shared with the class.

Submit votes independently and check eligibility carefully. The purpose is to recognize clear explanations and meaningful technical contributions through valid selections.

## 7. After the poll

After the results and any finalist presentations in the main Zoom room, join the **original project group's Zoom breakout room** for Sprint 2 planning. Use the Jigsaw findings to review the Product Backlog and plan the next working increment.
