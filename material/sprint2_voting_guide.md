# Sprint 2: Jigsaw Voting Guide

## 1. Check your Jigsaw group list before voting

Record the project group numbers represented in your assigned mixed-group Jigsaw group:

- **Project group you represent:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 1:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 2:** `[Group #]`
- **Other group 3:** `[Group #]`

If your Jigsaw group had three participants instead of four, list only the groups actually represented.

## 2. Zoom poll question 1: Best Presentation

**Question type:** Single choice — select **exactly one** group.

> **Which project group delivered the best Sprint 2 presentation in your assigned Jigsaw group?**
>
> Consider the working AI assistant, technical explanation, demonstration, MCP and interface integration, testing and evaluation, Scrum process, and answers to questions.

Choose one eligible group based on the presentations heard in your assigned Jigsaw group.

## 3. Zoom poll question 2: Concept Connectors

**Question type:** Multiple choice — the required number of selections depends on your Jigsaw group size:

- **Four-person Jigsaw group:** Select **exactly two distinct** other groups.
- **Three-person Jigsaw group:** Select **exactly one** other group.

> **Which project groups were represented by the strongest Concept Connectors in your assigned Jigsaw group?**
>
> Consider representatives whose questions or contributions connected course concepts, clarified technical choices, uncovered limitations, or helped others understand the projects.

Choose only eligible groups represented in your Jigsaw group. A useful question that clarifies how the system works contributes more to understanding than an overly complex one.

*(Note: Incomplete answers—such as selecting only one group when you were in a four-person Jigsaw group—are excluded during validation.)*

## 4. Final check before submitting

- [ ] Join the class Zoom session (with microphone muted and audio off) to access the poll.
- [ ] Select exactly **one group** for Best Presentation.
- [ ] Select exactly **two distinct groups** for Concept Connectors if you were in a **4-person** Jigsaw group, or **one group** if you were in a **3-person** Jigsaw group.
- [ ] Check that every selected group had a representative in your assigned Jigsaw group.
- [ ] Exclude the project group you represent from both questions.
- [ ] Compare every selected group number against your recorded Jigsaw group list.

## 5. Voting privacy

Individual choices are confidential among classmates; other students are not shown individual answers. The instructor can inspect identifiable records to check accidental self-votes, selections of groups absent from a Jigsaw group, and other voting errors.

> After the results and any finalist presentations, rejoin your **original project group** in class for **Sprint 3 planning**. Use your Jigsaw findings to review the Product Backlog and plan the next working increment.
